## About me

I'm a junior full-stack developer based in the Brasília metropolitan area, Brazil. I enjoy working where product thinking meets engineering: understanding the problem, defining clear contracts, and delivering reliable software that is useful beyond the code itself.

- 💻 Professional experience with **C#/.NET, Angular, Python, REST APIs, PostgreSQL, automated tests, and CI/CD**
- 📱 Currently building **multiplatform products** for web, Android, and iOS with React Native and Expo
- 🧱 Interested in **backend engineering, integrations, software architecture, and applied AI**
- 🌱 Deepening my practice in **Clean Architecture, DDD, automated testing, and AI-assisted engineering**
- 🤝 Open to **software development opportunities** and meaningful open-source collaboration

## Featured projects

### 🥗 [IronPlate](https://github.com/Fernandorsoul/ironplate)

A production-minded nutrition and athlete-tracking platform for **web, Android, and iOS**. It brings meal planning, macronutrients, workouts, body measurements, progress, and account privacy into one experience.

[![Live app](https://img.shields.io/badge/Open_live_app-0E7490?style=flat-square&logo=vercel&logoColor=white)](https://ironplate-phi.vercel.app/) [![Repository](https://img.shields.io/badge/View_repository-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/Fernandorsoul/ironplate) [![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-16A34A?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/Fernandorsoul/ironplate/blob/master/LICENSE)

`React Native` `Expo` `TypeScript` `Neon PostgreSQL` `Drizzle ORM` `Vercel Functions` `Jest` `Playwright`

> Highlights: JWT authentication, serverless API, versioned migrations, privacy controls, Brazilian food data, automated testing, and continuous delivery.

### 🌾 [AgronomoPlus](https://github.com/Fernandorsoul/AgronomoPlusFrontend)

A rural-management platform designed to support property and crop operations with weather integration, soil analysis, and data-informed recommendations.

[![Frontend repository](https://img.shields.io/badge/View_frontend-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/Fernandorsoul/AgronomoPlusFrontend)

`C#/.NET` `REST APIs` `PostgreSQL` `JWT` `DDD` `Clean Architecture` `React` `TypeScript`

### 🥋 BJJ Gym Management

A production application created to support day-to-day gym operations, including student records, safe data imports with duplicate-CPF validation, and scheduled email notifications.

`C#/.NET` `Angular` `Python` `PostgreSQL` `REST APIs`
